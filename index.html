Who we are

A network of Czech artisan-craftspeople

Arma Bohemia was founded in 1995 by swordmaster Jan Fantys and stuntman/cascadeur Jakub Malovany. The first shop was based in a 13th‑century stronghold in Romainmotier, Switzerland.

The Czech Republic has a long craft tradition of making arms and equipment. Today, more than 50 artisans work with Arma Bohemia, producing pieces based on old manuscripts, museum originals, private collections and scientific documentation — as well as the specific requirements of stage and film productions.

Roughly 80% of the workshop's output is custom work built to a client's own specification. The pieces presented in this catalogue are a representative selection, not the full range of what can be made.

Craft & materials

Working from the originals, not from imagination

"Using the ancient pieces as models, we retrieve the old technologies, instead of inventing new, non-historical production procedures."

A growing collection of originals

New copies are made from the workshop's own collection of period pieces, allowing accurate dimensions, material structure and weight.

Folded steel on request

For the most demanding clients, blades can be made from 100–400 year-old scrap iron, folded and forged in the way late-medieval steel was actually produced.

Roughly 80% custom work

Most pieces are made to a client's own reference: a manuscript illustration, a museum photograph or a technical drawing.

Have a reference of your own?

Send a manuscript excerpt, a museum photograph or a drawing — Arma Bohemia will reply with production possibilities, price estimate and delivery time.
