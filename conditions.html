Ordering & Conditions

How to order, pay, and receive a piece from Arma Bohemia.

How to order

Orders can be placed by email at info@armabohemia.cz or by letter to the workshop address. Arma Bohemia corresponds in English, German, Russian, Czech and French.

Please include in your order:

  • Your legal name, street address, e-mail address and phone number
  • The item code(s) and name(s), and quantities
  • Any specification: colour, size, blade sharpness, etc.
  • A desired delivery date, if relevant

Custom orders

About 80% of Arma Bohemia's production is custom work. Send a manuscript excerpt, a drawing or a museum photograph of the piece you'd like reproduced — the workshop will reply with production possibilities, a price estimate and a delivery time.

Once agreed, custom orders require a 50% deposit before production begins.

Prices

Because raw-material costs vary, all catalogue prices have informative value only and may be subject to minor changes. Please ask for the current price situation with each order.

Delivery & shipping

For stock items: postal / airmail delivery, or bulk personal delivery within Europe when possible (personal pickup is currently unavailable). The parcel is sent once the workshop receives a copy of the transfer confirmation by email or messenger.

Inside the EU, shipping costs vary between approximately 5 € for small items and 27 € for a heavy helmet or a longsword.

Warranty & returns

Pieces carry a two-year warranty against material and workmanship defects. To arrange a return, email or call within 14 days of receipt for a return authorisation, then return the item within 30 days for exchange or refund.
