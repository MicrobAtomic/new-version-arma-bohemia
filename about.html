The Workshop

Founded in 1995, Arma Bohemia brings together more than 50 Czech artisans making historically-informed reproductions of medieval and renaissance arms, armour and everyday objects.

Origins

From a Swiss stronghold to a Czech workshop network

Arma Bohemia, the Living-History & Reenactment workshop, was founded in 1995 by swordmaster Jan Fantys (pictured) and stuntman/cascadeur Jakub Malovany. The first shop was in Romainmotier, Switzerland, in the cellars of a 13th‑century stronghold.

The Czech Republic, a small country in the heart of Europe, has a long craft tradition of making arms and equipment. More than 50 men and women artisan-craftspeople now present their skills and experience through Arma Bohemia.

In the founders' own words

Arma Bohemia, the Living-History & Reenactment shop, was founded in 1995 by the swordmaster Jan Fantys and the cascadeur Jakub Malovany. The first shop was in Romainmotier (Switzerland), in a 13th-century stronghold. Now it is presented on this website.

The Czech Republic, a small country in the heart of Europe, has a long crafts tradition of making arms and equipment. Together, more than 50 men and women artisan-craftspeople are now presenting their skills and experience. Our goods are based on information from old manuscripts, various scientific documents, museums, special stage performing needs or film special effects.

Arma Bohemia cannot, of course, introduce itself — nor present our whole range of products and production capacities — through this website alone. In fact, about 80% of our production is made for clients' special wishes and specifications.

Do not hesitate to contact us: send a copy of a manuscript, your own drawing or a photograph, and you will obtain our answer with our production possibilities, price estimation and possible time of delivery.

This website will be periodically updated with new pictures and categories. You are welcome to visit our site regularly.

Sources & references

Working from documented originals

Products are based on information from old manuscripts, scientific documents, museum pieces and private collections — as well as the specific requirements of stage performances and film special effects.

Manuscripts & documents

Period illustrations and written sources inform the shape, proportions and construction of each piece.

Museum & private collections

Many reproductions are exact copies of originals held in institutions such as the Wallace Collection, London, or the Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York.

An in-house collection of originals

The workshop's own, constantly growing collection of period pieces allows accurate dimensions, material structure and weight for new copies.

Roughly 80% custom work

Most of what Arma Bohemia makes is not in this catalogue: it is built to a client's own reference and specification.

Materials & technology

Old technologies, not new shortcuts

"Using the ancient pieces as models, we retrieve the old technologies, instead of inventing new, non-historical production procedures."

Some clients expect maximum authenticity: the goods must not only have the right form and be made as much as possible by hand, but the material structure must correspond to museum pieces. Late-medieval steel was not as homogeneous as modern alloys — it was folded and forged together to obtain the best possible material.

For such pieces, the workshop uses 100–400 year-old scrap iron, folded in combination with the copies described above. Folded-steel blades can be made on request.
