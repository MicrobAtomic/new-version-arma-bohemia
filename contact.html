Custom orders

Sending a reference

About 80% of the workshop's production is custom-made. If you have a manuscript excerpt, a museum photograph or a technical drawing of a piece you'd like reproduced, send it by email — Arma Bohemia will reply with production possibilities, a price estimate and a delivery time.

Arma Bohemia corresponds in English, German, Russian, Czech and French.

Before you order

Prices
All catalogue prices are informative and may change with raw-material costs. Please confirm the current price when ordering.
What to include
Your name, address, email and phone number, the item reference(s) and quantities, any specification (size, colour, blade sharpness), and a delivery date if relevant.
Payment
SWIFT bank transfer (prices in EUR), PayPal on request, or cash.
